# Debug session: upcoming interview does not suppress feedback/follow-up reminders
Created: 2026-08-31T14:52:00Z
Session: task-209-2026-08-31-bugfix-1

## Phase 1 — Root Cause

### Error

Owner report (verbatim):

> "I have an interview on Thursday ... so I should not get a follow up for Vienna Insurance Group yet, since I have an interview scheduled up, so I can't know when to follow up yet"

The screenshot shows the Vienna Insurance Group lead in **Feedback deadlines**, due in nine days.

### Reproduction

1. Load `GET /api/jobs/feedback-due/` for the owner.
2. Observe that the measured VIG lead qualifies from `status='interview'` plus non-null `feedback_due_date`.
3. Compare reminder and interview fields read-only.

Released-runtime read-only output:

```text
matches=1
status=interview
interview_at=None
feedback_due=2026-09-09
open_followups=0
due_open_followups=0
message_count=0
pending_interview_suggestions=[]
writes=0 external_calls=0
```

Environment: released main runtime, scheduler disabled. Frequency: always for a dated feedback row; the endpoint has no interview-time exclusion.

### Suspect commits

- TASK-146 feedback-deadline endpoint — filters actionable status and non-null feedback date only.
- Existing stats/digest due-followup queries — filter incomplete due FollowUp rows only and likewise do not consider a newer upcoming interview.

### Instrumentation data

1. Feedback pane boundary: `JobLeadViewSet.feedback_due` filters only actionable status and `feedback_due_date__isnull=False`.
2. Due-followup count boundary: stats counts incomplete FollowUp rows due by local date with no interview exclusion.
3. Digest boundary: `followup_digest` uses the same due-date predicate without interview exclusion.
4. Measured-data boundary: VIG has no `interview_at`, no mailbox message/suggestion carrying an interview date, and no FollowUp row. The app therefore cannot derive Thursday from structured data.
5. Safety boundary: the owner explicitly says the current follow-up date is not knowable. Clearing that one invalid feedback deadline is truthful; inventing an interview time would fabricate data.

### Hypothesized root cause

Reminder eligibility ignores future `interview_at`, while the reported VIG row also lacks any structured interview timestamp, leaving its old feedback date eligible despite the owner's newer real-world interview. · Confidence: high

## Phase 2 — Pattern

A later scheduled event supersedes an earlier waiting deadline, but independent date fields are being evaluated in isolation. The same rule belongs at every due-reminder query boundary, not only in the screenshot's frontend.

The measured row additionally demonstrates a data-completeness edge: automatic suppression can only use a timestamp the app stores. A safe repair clears the deadline the owner says is unknowable; it must not guess Thursday's time from prose or inspect private note content.

## Phase 3 — Impact

Affected paths:

- `JobLeadViewSet.feedback_due` and its query-count/date-boundary tests.
- stats `jobs_needing_follow_up` count.
- follow-up digest due-row and candidate-job queries.
- job follow-up action UI, which locally derives `dueFeedback`/`hasDueFollowup`.
- The exact VIG `feedback_due_date` value, through one guarded owner-authorized repair.

Job/board/interview visibility, stored future interview timestamps, completed follow-ups, and unrelated leads must remain unchanged.

## Phase 4 — Solution

1. Exclude jobs with `interview_at > timezone.now()` from feedback-due, due-followup count, and digest due-reminder queries.
2. Apply the same future-interview check in the job-detail follow-up action so stale client state cannot re-expose the action.
3. Keep the boundary strict: equal/past interview timestamps are no longer upcoming and normal reminder eligibility resumes; null timestamps remain unaffected. Django's aware `timezone.now()` uses the configured timezone correctly at display/date boundaries.
4. Add synthetic future/equal/past/null tests and prove querying does not modify stored dates or remove the lead from jobs/upcoming-interview stats.
5. Guardedly clear only the measured VIG feedback deadline after confirming its interview timestamp is still absent; do not fabricate `interview_at`.

## Resolution

Implemented strict future-`interview_at` suppression in feedback deadlines, due-followup stats, digest/reconciliation candidates, confirmation, and the job-detail action. Added synthetic future/equal/past/null/no-mutation/visibility tests. The exact VIG deadline was cleared under the measured guard (`feedback_due_date=2026-09-09`, `interview_at IS NULL`); no interview time was fabricated and no Gmail/external call occurred. Verification: 1,044 backend tests, 202 frontend tests, production build, and focused 23-backend/5-frontend checks passed.
